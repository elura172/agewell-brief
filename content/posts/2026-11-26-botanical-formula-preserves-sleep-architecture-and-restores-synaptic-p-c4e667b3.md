Oral administration of Zhinao Capsules re-establishes neuron-microglia communication and clears amyloid burden in transgenic mice.

To test how traditional herbal mixtures influence neurodegeneration, researchers administered Zhinao Capsules, an eight-ingredient botanical preparation derived from the classical formulation Fu Lao Wan, to six-month-old APP/PS1 transgenic mice for twenty-eight days. The transgenic animals, which model human Alzheimer's disease pathology, displayed severe cognitive deficits, fragmented sleep-wake cycles, amyloid-beta accumulation, and synaptic protein loss. Daily oral gavage of the botanical extract at doses up to 0.936 grams per kilogram restored normal sleep distribution and spatial learning performance while suppressing cortical amyloid deposits and glial overactivation. At the cellular level, the treatment upregulated hippocampal CX3CL1 and CX3CR1 expression, restoring the biochemical handshake between neuronal fractalkine and microglial receptors. In parallel, in vitro co-culture experiments using Transwell plates demonstrated that recombinant CX3CL1 mirrored the protective phenotypes, whereas genetic knockdown of microglial Cx3cr1 partially attenuated the anti-inflammatory and synaptic rescue effects of the botanical mixture.

These findings position intercellular fractalkine signaling as a functionally relevant pathway through which complex botanical preparations modulate neuroimmune balance and preserve synaptic structural proteins such as SYN and PSD-95. However, the evidentiary boundaries of the study remain constrained by its animal model design. Surrogate marker measurements of molecular signaling pathways and murine behavioral assays do not measure tissue-specific botanical, anti-inflammatory, joint, enzymatic, or mucosal benefits in humans, and the single-time-point plasma mass spectrometry was qualitative rather than a definitive pharmacokinetic profile of maximum concentration.

## Key takeaways

- Oral administration of Zhinao Capsules improved abnormal sleep-wake distribution and cognitive performance in transgenic mice.
- Treatment reduced cortical amyloid-beta immunoreactivity and glial reactivity while preserving dendritic architecture and synaptic proteins.
- Upregulation of hippocampal CX3CL1/CX3CR1 signaling improved neuroimmune balance, with genetic knockdown showing partial pathway dependence.
