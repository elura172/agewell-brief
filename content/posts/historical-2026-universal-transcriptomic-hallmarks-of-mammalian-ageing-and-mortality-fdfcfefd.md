A multi-species transcriptomic analysis reveals conserved ageing signatures and modular clocks, linking inflammation, mitochondrial function, and chromatin dynamics to mortality.



## Three things to remember

- 11,000+ transcriptomes across 4 mammals identify conserved ageing genes.
- CDKN1A and LGALS3 protein levels correlate with human mortality.
- Caloric restriction and Klotho target mitochondrial and metabolic modules.
