New review maps how ROS-induced fibroblast senescence disrupts the extracellular matrix, fuels chronic inflammation, and accelerates aging across the body.



## Three things to remember

- ROS accumulation triggers fibroblast senescence and SASP-mediated inflammation.
- Senescent fibroblasts disrupt ECM homeostasis, reducing dermal thickness and elasticity.
- Nrf2, mTOR, TGF-β, and IGF-1 pathways regulate these aging processes.
