A sweeping review of over 350 studies hints that eating less protein could boost health and lifespan, but experts urge caution.




## Three things to remember

- Review of 350+ papers links protein restriction to slower aging.
- May improve metabolism and reduce cellular damage.
- Could extend lifespan in some cases, but human evidence is limited.
