A new review confirms that time-restricted eating triggers conserved survival pathways, extending lifespan and improving health across species.



## Three things to remember

- Fasting activates AMPK and sirtuins, boosting autophagy and mitochondrial health.
- Gut microbiome shifts produce butyrate, reducing inflammation and organellar apoptosis.
- Ancestral feast-famine cycles explain why periodic energy deprivation enhances resilience.
