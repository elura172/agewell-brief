A gut metabolite reopens ER–mitochondria–lysosome communication via calcium sparks, reviving mitochondrial quality control and extending healthspan in worms and human cells.



## Three things to remember

- UA releases ER calcium, driving DRP-1 fission and mitophagy.
- Calcium chelation blocks UA's benefits on muscle and lifespan.
- UA boosts mitochondrial biogenesis via CAMK2D and Nrf2.
