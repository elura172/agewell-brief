Spiky mats are popular, but evidence is thin. Here's what to know before you lie down.
## Three things to remember

- Mats inspired by ancient beds of nails, now a wellness trend.
- No compelling evidence they relieve pain, stress, or insomnia.
- Anecdotes suggest some people find them helpful, but risks include skin irritation.
