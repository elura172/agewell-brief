A new review maps how mitochondrial dysfunction spreads across brain cell types, revealing intercellular transfer as a metabolic lifeline and targeting mitochondria as a therapeutic frontier.



## Three things to remember

- Mitochondria integrate and amplify brain aging across neurons, glia, and vascular cells.
- Intercellular mitochondrial transfer offers a novel metabolic rescue mechanism.
- Clinical trials now test mitochondria-targeted interventions for cognitive decline.
