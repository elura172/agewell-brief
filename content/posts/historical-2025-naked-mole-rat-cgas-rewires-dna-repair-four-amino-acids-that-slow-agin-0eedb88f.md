A new study reveals how naked mole-rats evade a suppressive function of cGAS to enhance homologous recombination repair, offering a precise molecular lever for lifespan extension.



## Three things to remember

- Four amino acid changes in naked mole-rat cGAS weaken TRIM41 ubiquitination and P97 extraction.
- Prolonged chromatin retention boosts FANCI-RAD50 interaction, accelerating RAD50 recruitment to DNA breaks.
- This mechanism counteracts cellular and tissue aging, extending lifespan in laboratory models.
