New research reveals that bone marrow adipocytes flood neighboring cells with lipids, triggering mitochondrial dysfunction and senescence. A polycation-loaded framework interrupts this lipid crosstalk, restoring osteogenesis.



## Three things to remember

- Bone marrow adipocytes secrete lipids that impair mitochondrial function in neighboring cells.
- A biomimetic framework (CZP@LC) blocks lipid synthesis and secretion, preventing senescence.
- Interrupting lipid communication rescues osteoblasts and restores bone formation.
