A new review maps the cellular remodelling of fat tissue with age, linking senescent cells and immune shifts to disease risk, and weighs interventions from caloric restriction to senolytics.



## Three things to remember

- Ageing remodels fat tissue: adipocyte hypertrophy, progenitor exhaustion, immune infiltration.
- Senescent cells in fat secrete SASP factors, driving chronic inflammation and insulin resistance.
- Caloric restriction, metformin, GLP-1 agonists, senolytics may counter adipose ageing.
