A new study reveals that a burst of mitochondrial superoxide during development programs long-term nuclear envelope health by curbing unsaturated fat synthesis and lipid peroxidation.



## Three things to remember

- Reduced ETC activity preserves nuclear envelope morphology in aging worms.
- Developmental superoxide downregulates SBP-1, lowering unsaturated fatty acids.
- Lipid peroxidation control extends lifespan and eases progeria phenotypes.
