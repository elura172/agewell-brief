A moonlighting enzyme suppresses immunogenic mitochondrial double-stranded RNAs, balancing longevity and innate immunity in worms and human cells.



## Three things to remember

- Mitochondrial dsRNAs accumulate with age and trigger aberrant immune activation.
- FARS-1/FARSA binds dsRNAs via a novel domain, reducing their levels.
- Inhibiting FARS-1 boosts immunity but may trade off longevity.
