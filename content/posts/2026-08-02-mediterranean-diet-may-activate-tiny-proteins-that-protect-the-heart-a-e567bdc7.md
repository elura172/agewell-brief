New research suggests that closely following the Mediterranean diet may boost levels of humanin and SHMOOSE, proteins tied to heart and brain health.



## Three things to remember

- Higher diet adherence linked to increased humanin and SHMOOSE levels.
- Olive oil, fish, legumes, and fewer refined carbs appear key.
- Findings may guide future personalized nutrition for healthy aging.
