A new open resource catalogs neuropeptide sequences, structures, and spatial maps, offering a cross-species blueprint for studying peptide signaling and its role in metabolic and neural regulation.



## Three things to remember

- First centralized database for crustacean neuropeptides, spanning 29 species.
- Includes 1,364 peptides with physicochemical properties and predicted 3D structures.
- Links spatial localization and mass spectrometry imaging for functional mapping.
