A more accurate measure shows body fat doesn't rebound in kids; BMI rise may be muscle growth.



## Three things to remember

- Study challenges 42-year-old theory of childhood fat rebound.
- BMI rises around age 6, but body fat doesn't.
- Increase likely from muscle and lean tissue growth.
