Brief, low-dose CB2R agonists trigger an epigenetic switch that curbs plasticity and boosts tamoxifen response in organoid models.



## Three things to remember

- CB2R activation induces basal-to-luminal transition, reducing self-renewal and invasiveness.
- Differentiation persists under stress conditions that normally promote dedifferentiation.
- Epigenetic stabilization enhances tamoxifen sensitivity and limits resistant clone emergence.
