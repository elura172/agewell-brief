A systematic review maps senolytics, senomorphics, and metabolic mimetics—plus AI-driven discovery—to redefine healthspan.



## Three things to remember

- Senolytics like dasatinib + quercetin clear senescent cells.
- Senomorphics such as rapamycin suppress the SASP phenotype.
- Caloric restriction mimetics boost autophagy and mitochondrial function.
