A new study of 1,700 adults finds that frequent TV viewing in midlife is associated with smaller brain regions and more white matter damage in later years.



## Three things to remember

- Frequent TV viewers showed smaller memory and decision-making brain areas.
- White matter damage was more common in heavy TV watchers.
- Sitting alone wasn't the culprit; mentally engaging jobs were healthier.
