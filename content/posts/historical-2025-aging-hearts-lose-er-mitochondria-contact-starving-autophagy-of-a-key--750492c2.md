New research in mice shows that age-related loss of ER-mitochondrial contacts reduces mitochondrial PE synthesis, impairing autophagosome formation and causing giant dysfunctional mitochondria to accumulate. Boosting PISD activity via LACTB inhibition may restore mitochondrial quality control.



## Three things to remember

- Aged cardiomyocytes show reduced ER-mitochondria contacts, impairing PS transport and PE synthesis.
- PE deficiency disrupts autophagosome membrane formation, blocking autophagic flux and causing giant mitochondria.
- Inhibiting LACTB enhances PISD activity, restoring PE production and mitochondrial homeostasis in aging hearts.
