Nucleotide imbalance drives age-related inflammation by destabilizing mitochondrial DNA, activating cGAS-STING and SASP.



## Three things to remember

- Nucleotide imbalance increases ribonucleotide misincorporation into mtDNA.
- This triggers mtDNA release, cGAS-STING activation, and SASP.
- Adding deoxyribonucleosides suppresses the inflammatory response.
