A new study shows that transient injuries trigger persistent mtDNA mutations that impair energy metabolism and increase future kidney injury risk—but adenosine repletion may restore ATP via AK4.



## Three things to remember

- Transient injuries cause persistent mtDNA mutations that impair resilience.
- Mutations suppress nucleotide metabolism; adenosine repletion restores ATP via AK4.
- In 369,912 UK Biobank participants, mutation burden linked to kidney disease severity.
