New preprint suggests the brain encodes all moments equally, but retrieval favors information-rich, interconnected details—explaining gist memory without costly feedback.



## Three things to remember

- Uniform sampling model outperforms surprise-based theories in explaining narrative recall.
- Mutual information, not surprise, predicts which moments are remembered best.
- Findings align with energy-efficient encoding and ancestral oral traditions.
