A preprint reveals that Grpr+ spinal neurons contain two subtypes—Tac1+ for mechanical allodynia, Tac1− for itch—and shows genomic enhancers can silence each to reverse symptoms.



## Three things to remember

- Grpr+ neurons split into Tac1+ (pain) and Tac1− (itch) subtypes.
- Silencing Tac1+ reverses mechanical allodynia; Tac1− silencing stops itch.
- Inhibitory circuits and GRP sensitivity shape modality-specific output.
