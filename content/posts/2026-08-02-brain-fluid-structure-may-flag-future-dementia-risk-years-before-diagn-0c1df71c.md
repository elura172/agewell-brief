New research suggests that the structure of brain fluid could serve as an early warning sign for dementia, potentially enabling earlier interventions.



## Three things to remember

- Dementia affects over 57 million people worldwide.
- Biological markers may help estimate individual risk.
- Early detection could delay onset or slow progression.
