A two-molecule cocktail induces partial reprogramming, restoring genomic stability and reducing senescence, opening a new path to anti-aging therapy.



## Three things to remember

- Chemical cocktail improves genomic instability and epigenetic alterations in aged human cells.
- Two-molecule combination reduces cellular senescence and oxidative stress in vitro.
- In vivo treatment extends C. elegans lifespan and healthspan significantly.
