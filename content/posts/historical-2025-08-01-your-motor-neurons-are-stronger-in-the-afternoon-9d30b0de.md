New research shows that the spinal cord's built-in amplifier—persistent inward currents—boosts muscle output more in the late afternoon, revealing a circadian rhythm in neural excitability.



## Three things to remember

- PIC prolongation and amplification increase in the afternoon, enhancing motor neuron output.
- Reduced inhibitory input, not neuromodulatory drive, likely drives the diurnal effect.
- Findings suggest spinal gain modulation fluctuates with the circadian clock.
