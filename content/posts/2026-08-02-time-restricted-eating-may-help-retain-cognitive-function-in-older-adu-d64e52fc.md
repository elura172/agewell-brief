A small pilot study hints that time-restricted eating could offer modest cognitive benefits for older women with overweight or obesity.



## Three things to remember

- Pilot trial linked limited eating window to modest cognitive gains.
- Study involved older women with overweight or obesity.
- Findings are preliminary; dementia risk reduction not proven.
