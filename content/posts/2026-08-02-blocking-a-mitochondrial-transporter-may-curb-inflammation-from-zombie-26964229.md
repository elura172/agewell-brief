Researchers find that faulty mitochondria and epigenetic changes switch on inflammatory genes in senescent 'zombie' cells, hinting at a new anti-inflammatory target.



## Three things to remember

- Dysfunctional mitochondria help aging cells turn on inflammatory genes.
- This mechanism links cellular aging to chronic inflammation in age-related diseases.
- Blocking a mitochondrial transporter may curb inflammation without killing cells.
