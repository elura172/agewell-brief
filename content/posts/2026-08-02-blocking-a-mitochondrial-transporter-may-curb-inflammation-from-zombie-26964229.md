New lab research reveals how aging cells trigger inflammation and a potential way to stop it without killing the cells.



## Three things to remember

- Dysfunctional mitochondria help switch on inflammatory genes in aging cells.
- Blocking a mitochondrial transporter reduced inflammation in lab experiments.
- Findings suggest a new approach for healthier aging, but more research needed.
