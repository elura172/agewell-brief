A locust's antennal lobe—the insect's olfactory processing center—can distinguish biofilm from planktonic bacteria in under five seconds, offering a real-time biological gas sensor for infection detection.



## Three things to remember

- Locust olfactory neurons classify Pseudomonas and Staphylococcus biofilms with up to 96% accuracy.
- Neural population spiking patterns discriminate VOC mixtures within seconds, enabling real-time detection.
- Biological olfaction outperforms chemical analysis for complex odor mixtures, echoing ancestral diagnostic practices.
