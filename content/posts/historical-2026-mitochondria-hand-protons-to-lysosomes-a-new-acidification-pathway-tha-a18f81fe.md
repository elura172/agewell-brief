A conserved mechanism shows mitochondrial proton export via membrane contacts drives lysosomal acidification; aging disrupts this coupling, but engineered links restore it.



## Three things to remember

- Mitochondria-lysosome contacts transfer protons to acidify lysosomes.
- Aging disrupts contacts, causing lysosomal de-acidification and senescence.
- Restoring contacts reverses senescence and restores autophagic flux.
