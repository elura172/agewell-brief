Genome-scale models and classifiers expose fatty acid oxidation and transport shifts, echoing Warburg's century-old insight.



## Three things to remember

- KNN and SVM hit ~0.98 accuracy, ROC-AUC 1.00.
- Fatty acid oxidation and extracellular transport fluxes diverge.
- Computational only; n=90 demands experimental validation.
