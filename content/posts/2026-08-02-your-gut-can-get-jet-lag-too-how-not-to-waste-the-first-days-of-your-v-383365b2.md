Long flights disrupt more than sleep—your gut bacteria may also feel the time change, leading to fatigue and digestive issues.
## Three things to remember

- Travelers often face fatigue, sleep problems, and low energy after long flights.
- Time zone changes may also affect the trillions of bacteria in your gut.
- These symptoms may stem from more than just classic jet lag.
