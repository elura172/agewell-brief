The agency's health-fraud list now includes pre-workout products, alongside earlier alerts for supplements marketed for sleep, skin, pain, and bodybuilding.

## Three things to remember

- FDA's list flags products with undeclared drug ingredients.
- Recent additions: PRE-FORMANCE BLACK and RAPTURE Preworkout.
- Earlier alerts cover sleep, skin, pain, and bodybuilding products.

## How to interpret it


### Historical Biophysics & Lineage
The FDA's alerts for hidden drug ingredients in supplements directly connect to Guyton's principles: undisclosed stimulants (e.g., amphetamine-like compounds) can elevate heart rate and blood pressure via sympathetic activation, increasing cardiac output and peripheral resistance. This aligns with Guyton's models of acute cardiovascular regulation. Kelsey's legacy is reflected in the FDA's current enforcement actions, which aim to protect public health by identifying and publicizing products that contain undeclared pharmaceutical agents, thereby preventing adverse effects that could range from hypertension to arrhythmias.

**Ancestral parallel:** Traditional lifestyle practices, such as the use of natural stimulants like caffeine from coffee or tea, have been part of human culture for centuries. These natural sources contain complex mixtures of compounds that are generally recognized as safe in moderate amounts. In contrast, the hidden drug ingredients in modern supplements represent a departure from ancestral patterns, as they are synthetic, potent, and undisclosed. The physical mechanism involves the same adrenergic pathways, but the dose and purity of synthetic compounds can lead to exaggerated responses, increasing the risk of cardiovascular events. This highlights the importance of transparency and moderation, values that were inherent in traditional consumption of natural stimulants.
