# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults entering or living through later life who want useful health and longevity reporting without being patronized. They value readable type, direct language, credible sources, and information that respects both their intelligence and their time.

## Product Purpose

Agewell Brief turns current health and longevity reporting into a concise daily edition: one substantial read and two quick signals. Success means readers understand what happened, why it matters, and where the information came from.

## Brand Personality

Clear, spirited, and respectful. The voice is confident without pretending certainty, engaging without manufacturing fear, and warm without becoming sentimental.

## Anti-references

Avoid institutional disclaimer language on brand surfaces, infantilizing references to age, fear-based health marketing, generic clinical wellness branding, and sensational claims that disguise advertising as reporting.

## Design Principles

- State the value positively instead of defining the brand by what it rejects.
- Treat older readers as capable adults with full lives and strong judgment.
- Make sources visible while keeping the reading experience lively.
- Lead with the useful signal, then add context where it genuinely changes meaning.
- Keep commercial material clearly disclosed and visually distinct from reporting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high contrast, generous type sizes and line height, clear focus states, comfortable tap targets, semantic structure, and reduced-motion support. Do not assume one reading style, health status, or level of technical familiarity based on age.
